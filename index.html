Meet Helios by Isaphia

Helios by Isaphia is a unified exposure-management platform that shows you everything an attacker can reach: your internet-facing footprint, the assets inside your network, the intelligence attackers already hold on you, and your cloud posture — all in one dashboard. Run it yourself as SaaS, or have our team operate it for you.

  • External ASM

    Continuously discovers and prioritizes every internet-facing asset you own — including the ones you forgot.

  • Internal ASM

    Lightweight collectors map what's reachable behind the firewall — the legacy systems attackers pivot to once they're in.

  • CTI

    Asset-aware threat intelligence — every indicator matched against your real inventory, not fifty thousand generic IOCs a week.

  • CSPM

    Cloud Security Posture Management across AWS, Azure, and GCP — misconfigurations, identity risk, and compliance drift, caught early.
